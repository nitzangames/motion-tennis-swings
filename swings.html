What’s actually different?

Floating hands carry the racket independently of the body. Flat and topspin share the same racket path. Slice lowers it, lob lifts it, and smash reverses it overhead. Backhand reverses the lateral sweep; serve adds toss preparation. Power currently affects ball flight, not the animation.
